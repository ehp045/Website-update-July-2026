A Few Things I've Been Thinking About

April 14, 2026 · 5 min read

A few loose notes that didn't turn into full essays on their own, but seemed worth writing down together.

Reading your own work out loud

The single most useful editing tool I own costs nothing and takes ten minutes: reading the whole piece out loud, start to finish, without stopping to fix anything. Every awkward sentence announces itself.

First lines

A first line doesn't have to be clever. It has to make one promise the rest of the piece intends to keep. That's a lower bar than most writers set for themselves, and a more useful one.
